
## ⚠️ RISQUES & MITIGATION

### Technical
**Risk :** Performance on low-end devices
**Mitigation :** Aggressive optimization, quality settings

**Risk :** HealthKit/Google Fit issues
**Mitigation :** Thorough testing, fallback to manual

**Risk :** Save data loss
**Mitigation :** Cloud save, frequent backups

### Business
**Risk :** Low retention
**Mitigation :** A/B testing, iterate fast

**Risk :** Poor monetization
**Mitigation :** Multiple revenue streams, test pricing

**Risk :** No virality
**Mitigation :** Built-in sharing, influencers, AR filter

### Market
**Risk :** Competition (Sims Mobile)
**Mitigation :** Unique IRL link, faster iteration

**Risk :** App Store rejection
**Mitigation :** Follow guidelines, legal review
